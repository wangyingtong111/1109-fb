Enhancements
............

- :class:`~imblearn.over_sampling.RandomOverSampler` now accepts a
  ``sample_weight`` parameter in
  :meth:`~imblearn.over_sampling.RandomOverSampler.fit_resample`. The weight
  of each source sample is split equally between all its copies so that the
  total weight associated with every source sample is preserved. The
  resampled weights are returned as a third output and are aligned with the
  rows of the resampled dataset. Samplers creating synthetic samples without
  a one-to-one correspondence with the source data raise a
  :class:`NotImplementedError` instead of guessing weights. The
  :class:`~imblearn.pipeline.Pipeline` forwards the resampled weights to the
  final estimator during ``fit``, leaving the prediction chain unchanged.
